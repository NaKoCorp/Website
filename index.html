// our first product

DogSecrets

🚧 In development · coming soon

DogSecrets will be an iOS app for storing your passwords, MFA codes, and passkeys, 100% local by default.

Need to sync across multiple devices? Set up your own backend (S3, database...) to back up, share, or access your vault wherever you want, without ever depending on our servers.

Secure by design

Your private keys never leave your device.

Biometric protection

Face ID / Touch ID to protect your accounts.

Passkey standard

Built on WebAuthn and FIDO2.

Open source

Transparent, auditable, community-driven code.

You own your identity

Full control over your passkeys and your data.

Local by default. Connected if you want.

step 1

Encrypted local vault

Your secrets are stored and encrypted directly on your iPhone, unlockable with Face ID or Touch ID.

step 2 · optional

Connect your backend

Set up your own storage: S3, database, or any other compatible backend.

step 3 · optional

Sync and share

Access your vault from multiple devices and share it if needed, all while staying in control.
